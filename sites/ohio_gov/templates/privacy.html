Privacy Notice and Policies

Ohio.gov is committed to protecting your privacy. This notice explains what information Ohio.gov collects when you visit, and how that information is used.

Information we collect

Ohio.gov automatically receives and records information from your browser, such as the pages you request, the time of the requests, and your browser type. We do not collect personally identifiable information unless you choose to provide it, for example when you create an OHID account, subscribe to alerts, or request the Ohio Travel Guide.

How we use information

Information you provide is used to deliver the services you request. Aggregated, non-identifying statistics may be used to improve the website.

Security

When you log in with OHID, your privacy, data, and personal information are protected by federal and state digital security standards.
