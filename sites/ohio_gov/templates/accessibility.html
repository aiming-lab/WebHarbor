Accessibility

The State of Ohio is committed to providing access to Ohio.gov for all visitors, including people with disabilities.

Standards

Ohio.gov follows the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. Pages are designed to work with screen readers, keyboard navigation, and other assistive technologies.

Report a problem

If you have trouble accessing any part of Ohio.gov, contact the Ohio Assistant or the agency that maintains the page and describe the problem you encountered.
