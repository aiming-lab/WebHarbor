What the Ohio Assistant Can Do

  • Find official Ohio.gov resources and services.
  • Point you to the right agency, board, or commission.
  • Answer common questions using the Help Center FAQs.

What the Ohio Assistant Cannot Do

  • It cannot access your personal accounts or benefits.
  • It cannot process applications or payments.
  • It cannot give legal advice.

Is the Ohio Assistant Generative AI?

The Ohio Assistant searches official State of Ohio content and returns links to the pages that answer your question. It does not write new content.
