Ohio for Responsible Gambling

Play responsibly. Get help if gambling stops being fun.

Ohio for Responsible Gambling brings together state agencies to promote responsible gambling and connect Ohioans with prevention and treatment services.

If gambling is causing problems for you or someone you know, call the Problem Gambling Helpline: 1-800-589-9966.
