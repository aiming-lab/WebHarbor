About the Scores

Tomatometer

The Tomatometer reports the share of critic reviews that are positive. A score of 60% or more is Fresh; a lower score is Rotten.

Popcornmeter

The audience score reports the share of positive audience ratings. Verified ratings and all-audience ratings are different groups.

Certified Fresh

This is a separate designation with review-count and critic-coverage requirements. A high percentage alone does not grant it. Missing scores are shown as unavailable, never as zero.
