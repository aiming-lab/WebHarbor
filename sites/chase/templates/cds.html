Certificate of Deposit FAQ

What is a CD?

A Chase Certificate of Deposit is a savings account that earns a fixed interest rate over a set period of time. Your rate is locked in when you open the account and won't change during the term.

What is the minimum deposit?

You can open a Chase CD with $1,000 or more. Existing Chase checking or savings customers can open a CD online; new customers can visit a branch.

What happens at maturity?

At maturity, your CD automatically renews into the same term at the then-current rate unless you make changes during the 10-day grace period after maturity.
