Trust at Instructure

Privacy, security, and transparency are foundational to how we build education technology.

Our commitments

Instructure products are built with privacy and security in mind from the first line of code. We hold ourselves to industry-leading standards so schools, universities, and organizations can trust us with their most important data.

Security

We maintain comprehensive information security programs aligned to recognized frameworks, with regular penetration testing, encryption in transit and at rest, and 24/7 monitoring.

Accessibility

Our products are designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards, and our accessibility team works continuously with customers to improve the learning experience for everyone.

Privacy

We comply with FERPA, GDPR, and applicable state privacy laws. We collect only the data required to deliver our services, and we never sell student data.
