Our Story

Public Storage is the world's largest owner and operator of self-storage, with more than 4,600 locations serving over 2.5 million customers across 41 states.

Trusted nationwide since 1972, we built our name on bright, clean properties and the famous orange doors. Today our wide range of unit sizes and features — from budget-friendly lockers to enclosed vehicle spaces — makes it easy to find storage that fits your needs, month to month, with no long-term commitment.

Every rental starts with a free reservation: hold your unit online with no payment and no obligation, then complete the rental in minutes online or at the property.
