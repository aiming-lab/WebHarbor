# BabyCenter mirror notices

This benchmark mirror is not affiliated with BabyCenter or Ziff Davis. The
BabyCenter name is used only to identify the public site whose information
architecture and workflows are represented. The mirror does not redistribute
BabyCenter text or photography.

Factual article text is quoted from pinned English Wikipedia revisions under
CC BY-SA 4.0. Each rendered factual page identifies its source article,
revision and licence. The tracked source records are under `source_data/`.

The seven photographs under `static/images/` are delivered through the paired
Hugging Face asset archive. Their authors, source pages, direct byte URLs,
licences and exact SHA-256 values are recorded in `asset_inventory.json`.
Three are CC0; the remaining files are CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 2.0 or CC BY 4.0.
The interface crops them with CSS but does not alter the stored bytes.

Community threads, benchmark accounts, saved items and tracker state are
synthetic benchmark data, clearly separated from sourced medical text.
