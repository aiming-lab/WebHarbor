Marriott Bonvoy® — Your Lifelong Travel Partner

Marriott Bonvoy is the award-winning travel program of Marriott International. Membership is free and gives you our best rates, free Wi-Fi, and member-only offers at participating hotels worldwide.

Member Benefits

  • Members-Only Rates: save at least 5% on most rates at participating hotels.
  • Free Wi-Fi: complimentary in-room internet access at participating properties.
  • Mobile Check-In & Key: check in, get your room-ready alert, and unlock your door with your phone at select hotels.
  • Member-Only Experiences: access to Marriott Bonvoy Moments — unique events you can bid on with points.
  • Points on Stays: earn up to 10 points per USD on qualifying spend.

Earn Points

Earn Marriott Bonvoy points on eligible hotel spend (up to 10 points per USD at most brands), on Marriott Bonvoy credit card purchases, and with travel partners around the world. Elite members earn a 20–75% points bonus depending on tier.

Redeem Points

Use points for free nights at participating hotels, Marriott Bonvoy Moments, room upgrades, and partner travel — or transfer them to frequent-flyer programs. Free Night Awards start at just 7,500 points per night at select properties.
