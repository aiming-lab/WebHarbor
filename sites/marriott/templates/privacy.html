Privacy

This site is an offline benchmark mirror of marriott.com built for the WebHarbor evaluation environment. It is not an official Marriott International product, and no real guest data flows through it.

What this mirror stores

Account records you create (name, email, profile details, saved hotels, payment method placeholders and reservations) live in a local, resettable database inside the benchmark container. Passwords are stored hashed. Payment method numbers are never stored — only the card type and last four digits, exactly as the reservation and account pages display them.

Your choices

You can update or remove your profile details and saved hotels from the account pages at any time, and cancel reservations from Find My Reservation. All data is erased whenever the environment resets its database to the seed state.

Contact

Questions about this benchmark environment can be raised with the WebHarbor maintainers; this page does not represent the privacy practices of Marriott International, Inc.
