Local benchmark terms

Purpose

This WebHarbor environment is provided for deterministic local software evaluation. It is not the official Drugs.com service and does not claim ownership of, affiliation with, or authorization from the external service.

Medical-use restriction

Fixture records, simulated reviews, generated prices, interaction results, and simulated news must not be used for diagnosis, prescribing, dose selection, treatment, emergency decisions, or verification of current regulatory status.

Local accounts and content

Accounts, medication lists, settings, reviews, contact receipts, and newsletter preferences exist only inside the resettable local benchmark database or signed browser session. A reset removes runtime changes.

External links

User-activated external links leave the local environment. Their content and availability are outside this benchmark.
