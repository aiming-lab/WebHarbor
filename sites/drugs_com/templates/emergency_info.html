Emergency-Use Restriction

Do not use this benchmark in an emergency. It has no emergency assessment, poison-control, adverse-event reporting, clinician contact, dispatch, or current public-service directory.

Required action outside this benchmark

Use the emergency telephone number and official poison-control or adverse-event reporting service for your current location. This repository does not reproduce telephone numbers, addresses, forms, or operating hours because it does not maintain evidence that those details remain current.
