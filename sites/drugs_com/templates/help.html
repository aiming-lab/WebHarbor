Frequently Asked Questions

I forgot my password. How do I regain access?

This offline benchmark mirror does not send password-reset email. For the demo account, use alice.j@test.com with TestPass123!. To restore all benchmark accounts and data, reset the drugs_com site through the WebHarbor control plane; otherwise, register a new local account.

How do I search for a drug?

Type the brand or generic drug name into the search box at the top of any page. As you type, suggestions appear with matching drugs and conditions. You can also browse alphabetically via the Drugs A-Z page, or use the A-Z strip in the header.

What is the drug interaction checker?

The Local Interaction Fixture Checker accepts two or more stored names and returns exact rows from an intentionally incomplete deterministic dataset. It does not establish real coverage or guidance.

How do I use the pill identifier?

Open the Pill Identifier and enter any combination of imprint code, shape, and color from the pill. The tool returns matching tablet or capsule images along with the drug name and dosage form.

Can I use this mirror as medical information?

No. This environment contains deterministic benchmark fixtures, simulated records, and no claimed medical review. Verify real information with current official labeling and a qualified healthcare professional.

Does the local contact form report a side effect?

No. The contact form creates only a session receipt and sends no report. Use current official reporting channels outside this benchmark for any real event.
