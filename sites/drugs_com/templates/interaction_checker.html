Local checker help

How do I use this fixture?

Enter at least two stored medication names, or use the inline add controls, and submit the form. The result page lists exact matching pair records.

What is outside the data scope?

The database is intentionally incomplete and has no source for exhaustive interaction coverage, clinical severity definitions, management advice, dose adjustment, or patient-specific guidance.
