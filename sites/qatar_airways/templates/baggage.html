Baggage guidelines

Carry-on baggage

Dimensions must not exceed 50cm (length) x 37cm (width) x 25cm (depth) — 20in x 15in x 10in. Economy: 1 piece up to 7kg. Business and First: 2 pieces up to 15kg total.

Checked baggage

A single piece cannot exceed 32kg (70lb). For piece-concept routes, dimensions per bag must not exceed 158cm total; for all other routes the total must not exceed 300cm.

Infants

Infants have their own checked baggage allowance, plus one baby stroller or collapsible carrycot at no additional cost. Infants travelling on a separate seat are also entitled to one car seat at no additional cost.

Privilege Club members

Silver, Gold and Platinum members are eligible for additional baggage allowance based on their membership tier and route: Silver 15kg or one piece, Gold 20kg or one piece, Platinum 25kg or two pieces.
