Privilege Club tiers

Our membership benefits are designed to suit the specific needs of our Privilege Club members in different tiers. The higher your membership tier, the more benefits you will enjoy.

Join now Try the Avios calculator
In addition to the tier qualification and retention criteria, you will need to have earned at least 20% of your Qpoints or have flown 4 sectors (within 12 months prior to retention) or 8 sectors (within 24 months prior to retention) on flights marketed* and operated** by Qatar Airways.
*Marketed refers to the airline whose flight number is specified on your booking. **Operated refers to the airline you are flying on.
Burgundy
oneworld tier: —
  • 100% mileage per family member on all flights eligible for earning Avios
  • Flexi awards
  • Save 10% on seat selection
  • Shop & pay with Avios at Qatar Duty Free
  • Free Starlink Wi-Fi on board
  • 10% off Al Maha services and lounges

To upgrade to Silver: earn 150 Qpoints within any 12-month period.

Silver
oneworld tier: Ruby
  • 15kg extra baggage allowance, or one piece (depending on your route)
  • 25% tier bonus on eligible flights by Qatar Airways
  • Priority stand-by, check-in and boarding
  • Save 20% on seat selection
  • Lounge access

To retain Silver: {{ tier_retain['Silver'] }}. To upgrade to Gold: earn 300 Qpoints within any 12-month period.

Gold
oneworld tier: Sapphire
  • 75% tier bonus on eligible flights on Qatar Airways
  • 20kg extra baggage allowance, or one piece (depending on your route)
  • Preferred seats
  • Lounge access
  • 40 Qcredits to redeem for upgrades, excess baggage and much more
  • Al Maha 'meet and assist' service (conditions apply)

To retain Gold: {{ tier_retain['Gold'] }}. To upgrade to Platinum: earn 600 Qpoints within any 12-month period.

Platinum
oneworld tier: Emerald
  • 100% tier bonus on eligible flights by Qatar Airways
  • 25kg extra baggage allowance, or two pieces (depending on your route)
  • Preferred seats
  • Lounge access
  • Al Maha 'meet and assist' service (conditions apply)

To retain Platinum: {{ tier_retain['Platinum'] }}.
