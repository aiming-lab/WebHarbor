Coupon Policy and Price Guarantee

Coupon Policy

Michaels coupons are available on the Michaels web site, in the Michaels mobile app, via direct mail and the Michaels email and SMS programs.

  • Coupons for AORPI (Any One Regular Price Item), A2RPI (Any Two Regular Price Items) and ERPP (All Regular Price Purchases) are each limited to one coupon per product and one coupon of each type per day.
  • Coupons are limited to stock on hand, void where prohibited, and valid only in the US.
  • Sale and clearance items, technology products (including sewing machines), and items identified as closeout, doorbuster or special buy are excluded from percent-off regular price coupons unless the coupon states otherwise.
  • Teacher, Military, Senior and other group discounts cannot be combined with each other; only one group discount may apply per purchase.

Low Price Guarantee

If you buy a qualifying item online on Michaels.com and then find the identical item for less at Michaels.com, an eligible online retailer, or in a Michaels printed or digital ad, we will match that price. If you find a current lower price within 7 days of your Michaels.com online purchase, contact Michaels.com Customer Service at 1-800-642-4235 and we will adjust your payment to the lower price. Stores are unable to adjust online purchases.

The following conditions and limitations apply:

  • The item must be the identical item — brand name, size, weight, color, quantity and model number.
  • The customer must inform a Michaels Team Member of the price at the time of checkout (for in store matches).
  • The price must be listed and valid at the time of match. We reserve the right to verify a competitor's advertised price and availability.
  • No rainchecks will be issued for items that are out of stock at Michaels.com, and we do not match competitors who are out of stock.
  • We cannot stack other coupons from Michaels or other retailers on top of prices reduced as a result of the Low Price Guarantee.
  • Price matches are limited to one per identical item per customer for online competitor matches.

Eligible online retailers: Athome.com, Hobbylobby.com, Homedepot.com, Kohls.com, Lowes.com, Officedepot.com, Popshelf.com, Staples.com, Target.com, Walmart.com.

Group Discounts

  • Teachers can enjoy an additional 15% Off Entire Purchase Including Sale Items. All teachers, staff and faculty Pre-K through Post-Grad within the US qualify; educator status can be synced to your Michaels Rewards account so the discount applies automatically.
  • Military: an additional 15% off Entire Purchase Including Sale Items for military members with valid military ID, synced through the Michaels Rewards verification process.
  • Seniors: customers 55 years or older enjoy an additional 10% off Entire Purchase Including Sale Items with valid ID or completed verification.
  • Group discounts cannot be combined with each other, and all are limited to stock on hand and valid only in the US.
