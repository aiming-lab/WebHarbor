WHY THE DOG?

The yellow Labrador on our signs? That’s Raising Cane — the original.

Founder Todd Graves and his yellow Labrador, Raising Cane, were together from the very first Restaurant in Baton Rouge. The dog was so central to Todd’s story that the company itself is named after him.

Today, Raising Cane (and every pup who followed) stands for the loyalty, energy and ONE LOVE® attitude of our Crew. You’ll find him on our signs, our sauce cups, our Cane’s Gear — and on the plush puppies in our merch shop.
