Go Cardless

Once your Caniac Club account has been registered, you can connect your Caniac Club account to your Raising Cane’s mobile and online ordering account to easily view and redeem available offers:

  1. Sign up or log into your Raising Cane’s account online or in our App.
  2. Go to profile and select My Account.
  3. Select Connect My CANIAC Club Card to link your account.

Your Caniac Club account email address must be the same as your Raising Cane’s account email.
