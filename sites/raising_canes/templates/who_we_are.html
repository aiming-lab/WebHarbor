WHO WE ARE

ONE LOVE® — quality Chicken Finger meals, served better than anyone else.

Raising Cane’s was founded by Todd Graves in 1996 in Baton Rouge, LA. We are a Restaurant company that has ONE LOVE® — craveable Chicken Finger meals. Cane’s is known for its great Crew, cool Culture and active Community involvement.

Our Vision is to grow Restaurants, serving our Customers, all over the world and be the brand for craveable Chicken Finger meals, a great Crew, cool Culture and Active Community Involvement.
