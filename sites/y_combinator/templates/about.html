About Y Combinator

Y Combinator (YC) is a technology startup accelerator launched in March 2005. It has been used to launch more than 4,000 companies, including Airbnb, Coinbase, Cruise, DoorDash, Dropbox, Instacart, Quora, PagerDuty, Reddit, Stripe, and Twitch.

The YC Model

YC created a new model for funding early stage startups. Twice a year we invest in a large number of startups.

Our Mission

Our mission is to enable more people to start startups. We believe that startups are a great way to solve problems and create value in the world.
