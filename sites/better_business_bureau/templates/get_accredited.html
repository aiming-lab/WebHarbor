BBB Accreditation

BBB Accreditation is an honor - and a commitment. Accredited businesses support BBB's mission of advancing marketplace trust.

How are BBB ratings calculated?

BBB ratings range from A+ to F and represent BBB's degree of confidence that a business operates in a trustworthy manner. Ratings consider the business's complaint history, type of business, time in business, transparent business practices, and any known government actions affecting the business's marketplace conduct.

Standards for Trust

  • Build trust - establish and maintain a positive track record in the marketplace.
  • Advertise honestly - adhere to established standards of advertising and selling.
  • Tell the truth - honestly represent products and services, including clear disclosures.
  • Be transparent - openly identify the nature, location, and ownership of the business.
  • Honor promises - abide by written agreements and spirit of representation.
  • Be responsive - address marketplace disputes quickly and professionally.
  • Safeguard privacy - protect consumer data collected during transactions.
  • Embody integrity - approach all business dealings with integrity and good faith.

Apply for BBB Accreditation

To apply for BBB Accreditation, a business must have been operational for at least 90 days, meet the Standards for Trust, maintain a positive marketplace record, and be in good standing with BBB. Apply through your local BBB.
