About BBB

Better Business Bureaus are private, nonprofit organizations that have been advancing marketplace trust for over 100 years.

Our mission

BBB's mission is to be the leader in advancing marketplace trust. We do this by setting standards for marketplace trust, encouraging and supporting best practices, celebrating marketplace role models, and addressing substandard marketplace behavior when appropriate.

What we do

  • Business profiles: BBB maintains reports on millions of businesses, including complaint history, customer reviews, and BBB ratings.
  • Dispute resolution: BBB offers complaint and dispute support for consumers and businesses, free of charge.
  • Scam Tracker: BBB collects and publishes consumer reports of scams and fraud across North America.
  • Accreditation: Businesses that meet BBB's Standards for Trust may apply for BBB Accreditation.

BBB Standards for Trust

The Standards for Trust are a comprehensive set of best practices for how businesses should treat the public. They synthesize fundamental ethical business principles with important consumer expectations regarding conduct in the marketplace.

Complaint Acceptance Guidelines

BBB can help with most marketplace disputes, but there are disputes BBB cannot help with, including: disputes that are or may become the subject of litigation; employer-employee disputes; matters already decided by or pending before a court or government agency; complaints about the prices a business charges (except allegations of misrepresentation), and disputes between businesses that involve business-to-business transactions.

Terms of Use

Use of this site is subject to BBB's Terms of Use. BBB Business Profiles are provided solely to assist you in exercising your own best judgment. Complaint information is covered by a three-year reporting period.
