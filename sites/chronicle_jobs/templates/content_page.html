{% if slug == "about-us" %}

Chronicle Jobs is the job board of The Chronicle of Higher Education, the leading source of news and advice for college and university faculty members and administrators. Every month the site connects thousands of jobseekers with positions at colleges, universities, and organizations across the United States and around the world.

The Chronicle of Higher Education is based at 900 19th Street, N.W., 6th Floor, Washington, D.C. 20006. Job listings are posted by employers and powered by Madgex job board software.

For questions about the site, contact us.

{% elif slug == "privacy-policy" %}

Chronicle Jobs collects the information you provide when you create an account, upload a resume, set up a job alert, or apply for a job: your name, email address, resume details, and application history. We use this information to operate your account, send you the job alerts you request, and let employers review the applications you submit.

We do not sell your personal information. You may request a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account by contacting the team. Applications are shared only with the employer you applied to. Job alerts can be unsubscribed at any time.
