About Dillard's

Dillard's, Inc. is one of the largest fashion retailers in the country. The company was founded in 1938 by William Dillard and is headquartered in Little Rock, Arkansas. Today the company operates nearly 300 stores across 29 states, all with one nameplate - Dillard's.

Cutting-edge fashion and superior quality are the hallmarks of our exclusive products. We are committed to delivering value, selection, and an exceptional shopping experience in every community we serve.
