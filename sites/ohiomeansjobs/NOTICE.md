# OhioMeansJobs mirror media notice

This offline benchmark mirror is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
State of Ohio, the Ohio Department of Job and Family Services, or
OhioMeansJobs. Photography and brand imagery in the paired
`ohiomeansjobs.tar.gz` asset archive was collected from the public
OhioMeansJobs / Ohio.gov content delivery endpoints
(ohiomeansjobs.ohio.gov, dam.assets.ohio.gov, and the OhioMeansJobs job
board's securemedia CDN) and remains the property of its respective
owners. OhioMeansJobs names and marks are used to identify the mirrored
service.

Job postings (titles, employers, locations, job types, reference codes,
posting dates, and full descriptions), the county job-center directory,
news items, help-center articles, and the state agency roster were
captured from the live public site on 2026-09-24 and are frozen as of
that date. Industry, salary-band, education, and certification
classifications follow the real facet taxonomy published by the
upstream job board (omjindustry, saltyp, lv, certification criteria);
per-job values are derived from each posting's title and stated
requirements, and from pay figures stated in the posting text whenever
present.

Benchmark accounts, saved jobs, saved searches, applications, resumes,
cover letters, career plans, and contact submissions are local test
fixtures. Actions in this mirror do not contact the live
OhioMeansJobs service.
