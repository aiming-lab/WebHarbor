Whistleblower Program

The SEC pays monetary awards to eligible whistleblowers who provide high-quality, original information that leads to a successful enforcement action.

How the process works

Individuals who report possible securities law violations through the SEC's Tips, Complaints & Referrals (TCR) form may be eligible for an award if their original information leads to monetary sanctions over $1 million.

Submit a Tip

Awards and protections

Awards can range from 10 to 30 percent of the monetary sanctions collected. The law protects whistleblowers from retaliation by employers, and the SEC can take action against anyone who interferes with a whistleblower's communications.
