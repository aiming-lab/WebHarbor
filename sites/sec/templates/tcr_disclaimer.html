Tips, Complaints & Referrals (TCR) Form

Before you begin: information you submit through this form is presumed confidential under the Whistleblower Program.

The SEC's Whistleblower Program rewards high-quality original information that leads to enforcement actions with monetary sanctions exceeding $1 million. To be an eligible whistleblower, you must submit your tip through this official channel.

Keep in mind:

  • You do not need to be an insider or have a lawyer to submit a tip.
  • Deliberately making false or misleading statements may itself violate the law.
  • Submitting a tip does not create an attorney-client relationship with the SEC.
