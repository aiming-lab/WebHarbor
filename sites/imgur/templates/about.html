Up, Up, And Away — Our Journey

  • 2020 — Imgur launches Imgur Emerald, a subscription product that offers users an ad-free experience and exclusive perks.
  • 2019 — Imgur celebrates its 10th birthday and reaches 300 million people per month.
  • 2018 — Imgur now supports video uploads, and is listed among the “100 websites that shaped the internet as we know it” by Gizmodo.
  • 2017 — Imgur turns users into curators with Favorite Folders, and reaches 250 million people per month.
  • 2010 — Imgur launches as a simple, fast image host that “slices and dices” images for the web.
