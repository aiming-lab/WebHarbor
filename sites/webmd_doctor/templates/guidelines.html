WebMD Reviews Guidelines

By submitting a review on the WebMD Care physician directory you agree to the Terms and Conditions and to the following review guidelines:

  1. Your review must describe your own experience with the provider: how the visit went, whether your questions were answered, the wait time and the follow-up. Reviews of a provider you have not seen, or written on behalf of a provider, are not accepted.
  2. Your review must not include personal health information about other people, or the full names of office staff.
  3. Your review must not contain profanity, threats, discriminatory language or anything unlawful, defamatory or harassing toward any person or entity.
  4. Your review must not include advertisements, solicitations or "spam" that detracts from other patients' experience of the site.
  5. Your review must carry an overall star rating from 1 to 5 and a "did well" or "needs improvement" answer for each of the seven Patients' Perspective criteria.
  6. Your review must contain at least 20 characters of written feedback so other patients understand the rating.
  7. A newly submitted review appears on the provider's profile to its author with the status Pending review until the moderation team checks it; published ratings and counts on a profile do not change until a review is approved.
  8. Providers cannot edit or remove reviews. They may reply to a published review through their claimed profile, and readers' helpful votes and flags are used to prioritise moderation.

WebMD Care reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to remove reviews that do not meet these guidelines or the terms set forth in the Terms and Conditions.
