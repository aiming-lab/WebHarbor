Investor relations

Ryanair DAC is Europe's largest low-cost airline group. Share price, results and shareholder information are published through the investor relations portal.

Financial calendar

Half-year and full-year results are announced in the autumn and spring respectively, followed by quarterly traffic statements.

Shareholder information

Ordinary shares are listed on the principal stock exchanges in Dublin and London; shareholder notices are published with each results announcement.

Company reports

The annual report and interim results, including traffic, fare and profitability statistics, are available from the investor relations portal.
