Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 24 March 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Ryanair collects and uses personal data when you use this website and book flights.

Data Controller

Ryanair DAC, Airside Business Park, Swords, Co. Dublin, Ireland is the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

What personal data we collect

Account details (name, email address, phone number), passenger details required to fly (name, travel document information), booking and payment information (card type, last four digits, billing address), and contact preferences.

What we use your personal data for

To operate your booking and your customer account, to process payments, to provide check-in and boarding passes, to send operational flight updates for the contact details you provide, and to improve the website.

How long we keep your data

Booking records are kept for as long as needed to provide the flight and handle refunds or claims, and afterwards only for the period required by aviation and tax law.

Security of your personal data

Personal data is stored securely and access to it is limited to the purposes described in this policy.

Sharing and transferring your personal data

Data is shared only with the service providers needed to operate your booking, such as payment processors, and where required by law.

Cookies and site tracking

This website uses cookies to keep you signed in to your account and to remember your search preferences. See the cookie preferences link in the footer.

Data Protection Officer

Ryanair's Data Protection Officer can be contacted through the help centre for any privacy question about your data.

Your Data Protection Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may object to or restrict certain processing of it.

Changes to Privacy Policy

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
