About Ryanair

Ryanair is Europe's largest low-cost airline, operating more than 2,400 routes to over 20 countries from major bases in Ireland, the United Kingdom, Italy, Spain, Poland and beyond.

Founded in 1985 with a single 15-seater aircraft flying the Dublin–London route, Ryanair now operates one of the youngest fleets in Europe: hundreds of Boeing 737 aircraft flying short-haul point-to-point routes that connect regional airports to the continent's biggest cities and holiday destinations.

Ryanair's low fares are built on point-to-point flying, fast aircraft turnarounds, a single aircraft family and the direct online distribution channel you are using right now — book direct for the lowest fares.
