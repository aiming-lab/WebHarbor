Careers at Ryanair

Ryanair recruits pilots, cabin crew, engineers and head-office teams across its European bases. Roles are advertised by the team and base on the careers portal.

Cabin crew

Cabin crew are recruited for each base in Ireland, the UK, Italy and Spain, with training provided at the airline's training centres.

Flight crew

Type-rated and non-type-rated Boeing 737 pilot programmes run throughout the year, including cadet entry schemes.

Engineering and operations

Licensed aircraft engineers and operations teams keep one of Europe's youngest fleets flying from maintenance bases across the network.
