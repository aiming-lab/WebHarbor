Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to join long-term?
Membership is designed to be flexible. Enjoy exclusive wine selections delivered throughout the year, with the freedom to manage your membership whenever you need.
How much is the first shipment?
Your introductory shipment includes 12 carefully selected bottles for just $99.99 + applicable tax, with free shipping. After your first shipment, your membership renews quarterly at $149.99 + applicable tax, with free shipping. It's the perfect way to discover exceptional wines and experience the benefits of membership.
What happens after the first shipment?
After your introductory shipment, you'll continue receiving 12 bottles approximately every 13 weeks as part of your ongoing membership. Each shipment is $149.99 plus applicable tax, billed when your order ships.
Can I choose the type of wine?
Yes, you can choose: Mixed (12 unique wines, 1 bottle each), All Reds or All Whites (6 unique wines, 2 bottles each).
How often will I get wine?
Shipments arrive approximately every 13 weeks, making it easy to enjoy new selections throughout the year.
Can I skip or cancel shipments?
Absolutely! Shipments can be skipped or canceled anytime. The club is designed to work around your schedule, so you always have control over what and when you receive.
What if I don't like a wine?
We want every shipment to be enjoyable. If you have questions or concerns about a wine you've received, please contact Customer Support at (855) 966-2224 and we'll be happy to help.
Is shipping included?
Yes. Shipping is included for all club shipments, delivered to your home or to a convenient pickup location. The wines arrive safely and ready to enjoy.
Can this offer be combined with other promotions?
The Wine Club offer may not be combined with other offers and the membership is limited to one per household.
How can I skip or cancel my membership?
You may skip a shipment or cancel at any time by contacting Customer Support at (855) 966-2224 or by logging into your account to manage your club preferences.
