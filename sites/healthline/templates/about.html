About Healthline

Healthline is one of the most-visited health information websites, providing evidence-based health and wellness content. Every article is written and medically reviewed by qualified professionals — registered dietitians, physicians, nurses, pharmacists, and licensed therapists.

Our content spans health conditions, nutrition, mental health, fitness, drugs and medications, and everyday wellness. We aim to make complex medical information clear, accurate, and actionable.
