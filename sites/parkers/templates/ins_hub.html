What are car insurance groups for?

Insurance groups are used by insurers to help set premiums. Cars in group 1 are the cheapest to insure and those in group 50 the most expensive. The group rating is based on the car's value, performance, repair costs, safety features and security.

When choosing a car it's worth checking its insurance group — a difference of just a few groups can add hundreds of pounds to a young driver's premium.
