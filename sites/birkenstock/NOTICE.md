# BIRKENSTOCK mirror attribution

This is an offline benchmark mirror of the BIRKENSTOCK US storefront.
Product photographs, branding, catalog and policy text, and the DIN Next CYR
fonts belong to their respective rights holders. Source URLs and capture hashes
are recorded in `asset_inventory.json` and `provenance.json`; this notice does
not grant a license to those materials or imply endorsement by BIRKENSTOCK.

The catalog and store snapshot was captured on September 21, 2026. Benchmark
users, orders, carts and review prose are fixtures, not real customer records.
For corrections or removal requests, open an issue in the WebHarbor repository
identifying the file and its recorded source URL.
