The Original Birkenstock Footbed

A Legendary Design

The heart of all models is the original BIRKENSTOCK footbed. The construction, which has been thought out to the smallest detail and makes it feel like you're standing in sand, helps your feet feel as comfortable as possible for hours on end. This supports health and promotes wellbeing.

Why it works

  • More room for toes: allows toes to move freely and grip naturally.
  • Contoured arch support: the footbed is anatomically shaped to support the natural foot.
  • Cork-latex core: a sustainable material extracted from the bark layer of the cork oak. This natural product is insulating and offers very good cushioning.
  • Deep heel cup: gives the foot a firm hold and keeps it in its natural position.

The more you wear your BIRKENSTOCK footbed, the more it adapts to the shape of your foot.
