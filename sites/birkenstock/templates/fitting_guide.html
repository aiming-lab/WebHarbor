Find your European size

All our footwear is listed in European sizes. To find yours, use the formula below or reference your U.S. shoe size in our conversion chart.

Women's: U.S. size + 31 = EU size

Men's: U.S. size + 33 = EU size

Width

Regular/Wide fits higher-volume feet. Medium/Narrow fits lower-volume feet. If you can fit your finger between the strap and your foot, the width is right for you.
