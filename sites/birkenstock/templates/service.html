Payment methods

We offer various payment methods in the BIRKENSTOCK online shop:

PayPal: PayPal allows you to pay easily and securely. You will be forwarded to PayPal during the ordering process. If you are already registered with PayPal, you can log in there and pay via your PayPal account. PayPal also offers the option for processing a payment as a guest without registering.

Credit card: You can conveniently pay in the BIRKENSTOCK online shop by credit card. We currently accept American Express, Master Card, Discover Card and Visa Card. An integrated SSL procedure ensures the security of the data transmitted during credit card payments.

Apple Pay: Apple Pay lets you make secure purchases on the web using your Apple devices.

Afterpay: If you believe there is fault or concern with your Afterpay purchase payment, please contact Afterpay customer support at info@afterpay.com.

Google Pay: Google Pay lets you make secure purchases on the Google Chrome browser and Android devices.

Shop Pay: Shop Pay is a convenient way to pay online, saving your shipping and payment details for faster checkout.
