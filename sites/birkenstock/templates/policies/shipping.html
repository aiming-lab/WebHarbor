Shipping Policy

Shipping method and delivery times

We offer the following shipping methods:

GROUND SHIPPING: Transit time will vary based on your location and takes between 2 and 5 business days.

2-DAY SHIPPING: Order must be placed Monday-Friday by 12pm EST to ship same day.

We only ship to the continental U.S., Alaska and Hawaii. We do not ship internationally. We only ship to PO Boxes within the continental US and cannot ship to PO boxes in Alaska or Hawaii. Please also note that we do not ship on public holidays.

Free shipping applies with the purchase of 2 or more full-priced sandals.
