Return Policy

BIRKENSTOCK.COM/US RETURNS

Footwear and accessories may be returned or exchanged, free of charge, in original purchase condition and packaging, within 30 days of delivery with the exception of Sale items that are discounted 40% or more. Sale styles that are discounted 40% off or more are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged. All returns are subject to inspection. All packages, including original containers, must be unmarked and not defaced in any manner.

Products purchased on www.birkenstock.com/us must be returned online at our returns page. They cannot be returned to a BIRKENSTOCK store or authorized retailer.

If you would like to return an item, go to the returns page and follow the instructions.

If you would like to exchange an item, please call customer service at 844-505-4055 or email info@support.birkenstockusa.com and they will be glad to help you.
