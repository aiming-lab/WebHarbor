1774

1774 is BIRKENSTOCK's premium designer line - a platform for collaboration with the world's leading fashion houses, released in numbered, limited editions.

The line takes its name from the year the first Birkenstock registered as a shoemaker. Each collection pairs the original contoured footbed with elevated materials and runway-led design.

1774 pieces are sold exclusively through select partners and the official BIRKENSTOCK channels. Explore the current new arrivals for styles available on this site.
