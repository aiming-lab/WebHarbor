Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my BIRKENSTOCK size?

All our footwear is listed in European sizes. Women's: U.S. size + 31 = EU size. Men's: U.S. size + 33 = EU size. See the full Fit and Size Guide for the conversion chart.

What are your customer service hours?

Toll free calls Monday - Friday 9am to 9pm EST at (844) 505-4055, or email info@support.birkenstockusa.com.

How long does shipping take?

Ground shipping transit time varies based on your location and takes between 2 and 5 business days. 2-Day shipping orders must be placed Monday-Friday by 12pm EST to ship the same day. We ship to the continental U.S., Alaska and Hawaii only.

What is your return policy?

Footwear and accessories may be returned or exchanged, free of charge, in original purchase condition and packaging, within 30 days of delivery - with the exception of Sale items discounted 40% or more, which are final sale. See the full Return Policy.

How do I track my order?

Visit the Order Status page and enter your order number with the email address used at checkout.

How does BIRKENSTOCK VIP work?

It's free to join and you start with 25 points. You earn 1 point for every $1 spent, and 300 points can be redeemed for $10 off a purchase of $100 or more. See BIRKENSTOCK VIP for tiers and all the ways to earn.

Can I return an online order to a store?

Products purchased on www.birkenstock.com/us must be returned online at our returns page. They cannot be returned to a BIRKENSTOCK store or authorized retailer.

How do I exchange an item?

If you would like to exchange an item, please call customer service at 844-505-4055 or email info@support.birkenstockusa.com and they will be glad to help you.
